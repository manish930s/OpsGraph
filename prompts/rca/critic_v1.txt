Review the proposed Root-Cause Analysis (RCA) draft for potential logical flaws, citation hallucinations, or unsubstantiated assumptions.

{draft_rca}

### Evaluation Criteria
1. **Citation Alignment**: Check every citation key (e.g. `[CTX-...]`). Does it map exactly to the provided context? Report any hallucinated citation IDs.
2. **Factuality Check**: Identify any claims presented as observations that are not directly supported by the telemetry.
3. **Hypothesis Evaluation**: Are the proposed hypotheses logically sound based on the observations? Do they contradict any other telemetry?
4. **Distinction**: Ensure findings clearly separate observed facts from diagnostic assumptions.

### Output Schema Instruction
You must output a JSON object containing the following keys:
1. `response_id`: A unique stable string identifier.
2. `task_type`: Must be "critic".
3. `is_valid`: Boolean indicating if the draft has no hallucinations or factuality issues.
4. `findings`: List of critiques, logical errors, or factuality conflicts found in the draft.
5. `suggestions`: List of improvements to align the draft with raw evidence.
6. `decision`: Recommends the next state transition: "ACCEPT" (if RCA is solid), "CONTINUE_INVESTIGATION" (if critical gaps exist), "HUMAN_REVIEW" (if ambiguity is high or budget exhausted), "REJECT" (if draft is completely invalid).
7. `confidence_score`: Numeric float between 0.0 and 1.0 representing review confidence.
8. `evidence_gaps`: List of missing evidence gaps identified.
9. `reasoning_summary`: A concise summary of the critique reasoning.
10. `required_next_evidence_category`: The category of evidence needed next (e.g. "log", "metric", "trace", "deployment", "topology") or null.
